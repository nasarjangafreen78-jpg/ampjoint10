Refund & Returns Policy of AMP Joint-10™



## Our 60-Day Satisfaction Guarantee

At AMP Joint, your satisfaction is important to us. That's why every purchase is backed by our **60-day money-back guarantee**, beginning on the date your order is shipped.

This trial period gives you the opportunity to use AMP Joint as part of your daily routine and determine whether it meets your expectations. We encourage you to give the product sufficient time to experience its intended benefits before deciding whether a refund is necessary.

If, for any reason, you are not completely satisfied during the 60-day guarantee period, we'll gladly assist you with the return and refund process.

---

## Simple 3-Step Refund Process

To help ensure your refund is processed quickly and efficiently, please follow the steps below.

### Step 1: Verify Your Eligibility

Before requesting a refund, please confirm that your order is still within the **60-day satisfaction guarantee**.

You can verify your shipping date by reviewing your order confirmation email or the packing slip that was included with your shipment.

---

### Step 2: Contact Our Support Team

To begin your refund request, please email our customer support team using the subject line:

**Refund Request**

Including this subject line helps us route your request to the appropriate department for faster processing.

**Email:** [support@AMPJoint.com](mailto:support@AMPJoint.com)

---

### Step 3: Return Your Product

Please return **all bottles**, whether they are used, unused, or unopened, to the return address listed below.

Customers are responsible for return shipping costs. For your protection, we strongly recommend using a shipping service that provides tracking.

Once your package has been shipped, please email us the tracking number so we can monitor your return and process your refund as quickly as possible.

Please include the following information inside your return package:

* Your full name
* Your order number (available in your order confirmation email)
* The email address used when placing the order

## Refund Processing

After your returned items have been received and inspected, we will process your refund in accordance with our 60-day satisfaction guarantee.

You will receive a confirmation email once your refund has been issued.

Depending on your financial institution or payment provider, it generally takes **5–10 business days** for the refunded amount to appear on your account or billing statement.
