Privacy Policy for AMP Joint-10™



**Last Updated: May 19, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how AMP Joint collects, uses, stores, shares, and safeguards your information when you visit our website, place an order, contact our customer support team, or interact with our products, services, advertisements, emails, SMS messages, and other online features.

By accessing or using the AMP Joint website, you acknowledge that you have read and agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with any part of this Privacy Policy, please discontinue using this website.

## Information We Collect

We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide while visiting our website, placing an order, subscribing to updates, contacting customer support, participating in promotions, or otherwise interacting with AMP Joint.

The information we may collect includes:

* Full name
* Email address
* Phone number
* Billing and shipping address
* Payment-related information
* Order history and purchase details
* Customer support correspondence
* Any additional information you choose to provide

In addition, we may automatically collect certain technical and usage information when you access our website, including:

* IP address
* Browser type
* Device type
* Operating system
* Pages viewed
* Referring website or URL
* Time spent on individual pages
* Website usage and interaction data

## How We Use Your Information

AMP Joint may use the information we collect for purposes including:

* Processing and fulfilling your orders
* Providing customer support
* Sending order confirmations, shipping notifications, and account-related updates
* Responding to questions, requests, and customer service inquiries
* Improving our website, products, services, and overall customer experience
* Sending promotional emails, SMS messages, and marketing communications where permitted by applicable law
* Detecting and preventing fraud, unauthorized transactions, and prohibited activities
* Complying with legal obligations, regulations, and business requirements

## Email, Phone, and SMS Communications

By providing your email address or phone number, you consent to receive communications from AMP Joint regarding your purchases, account activity, customer support requests, product updates, promotional offers, and other relevant information.

You may unsubscribe from promotional email communications by following the unsubscribe instructions included within those emails. Promotional SMS messages may generally be stopped by replying **STOP**, where applicable.

Please note that even if you opt out of marketing communications, we may continue sending transactional or service-related messages regarding your orders, payments, shipping updates, or customer support requests.

## Cookies and Tracking Technologies

AMP Joint may use cookies, pixels, tags, analytics services, and similar technologies to:

* Operate and maintain the website
* Remember user preferences
* Analyze website traffic and performance
* Measure advertising effectiveness
* Improve user experience

Most web browsers allow you to control or disable cookies through browser settings. Please note that disabling cookies may affect certain website features and functionality.

## How We Share Information

AMP Joint does not sell your personal information for monetary compensation.

However, we may share information with trusted third-party service providers and business partners that assist in operating our business and delivering services.

These may include:

* Payment processors
* Shipping and fulfillment providers
* Customer support platforms
* Email and SMS communication providers
* Analytics and advertising partners
* Fraud prevention and security service providers
* Professional advisors, including legal, accounting, and compliance consultants

We may also disclose information when required by applicable law, legal process, governmental request, court order, or when we believe disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, customers, business operations, website security, or the safety of others.

## Payment Information

Payment transactions are processed through secure third-party payment processors.

AMP Joint does not intend to store complete credit card information on its own website servers. Payment providers collect, process, and store payment information in accordance with their own privacy policies and security practices.

## Data Security

We maintain reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Despite our efforts, no internet transmission or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Therefore, we cannot guarantee absolute security of your information.

## Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including order fulfillment, customer support, legal compliance, accounting, reporting, fraud prevention, and business recordkeeping requirements.

These rights may include:
